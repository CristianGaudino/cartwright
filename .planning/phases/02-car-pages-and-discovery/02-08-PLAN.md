Success: the item reads `Link copied` for 2 seconds, announced through an `aria-live="polite"` region, then the menu closes; rejection: the item reads `Could not copy the link` and WhatsApp still works. The menu closes on Escape, on an outside pointer down and after choosing WhatsApp, and returns focus to Share. Motion: 150ms opacity plus a 4px upward move, `ease-out`; opacity only under reduced motion. Clear timers on unmount. No logging.
