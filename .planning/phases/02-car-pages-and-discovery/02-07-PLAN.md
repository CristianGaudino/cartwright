## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `params.slug` and `params.model` -> provider | Untrusted route params |
| provider data -> HTML and JSON-LD | Make, model and body labels and car records rendered |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-20 | Tampering | route params | low | mitigate | Params are only compared against `taxonomy()` entries and passed to the provider as exact-match filter values; anything unknown calls `notFound()` before rendering; E2E covers the 404 cases |
| T-02-21 | Tampering | labels and car records in JSON-LD | medium | mitigate | `ItemList` renders through `JsonLd` (escapes `<`); labels render as React text; gate keeps `JsonLd.tsx` the only raw-HTML file |
| T-02-22 | Information disclosure | thin or empty pages indexed; dealer data on the demo | low | mitigate | Empty pages are noindex and out of the sitemap (D-08); the demo build is noindex everywhere (02-02) |
| T-02-23 | Information disclosure | sale dates on recently sold cards | low | mitigate | Cards use `ListingCard` (no date, 02-06); grep gate; E2E checks the raw HTML has no sale date |
| T-02-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | No package installed in this plan |
</threat_model>
