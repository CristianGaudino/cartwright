## Spacing Scale

Unchanged from Phase 1: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Touch targets 44px minimum, primary buttons 48px. Page content max width 1280px, gutters 16 / 24 / 32 at base / sm / lg.

New uses of the scale in this phase:

| Where | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Gap between page sections on a car page and landing page | 48px (2xl) |
| Gap inside a section (heading to content) | 16px (md) |
| Badge and counter inset from the photo edge | 16px (md) |
| Gap between thumbnails | 8px (sm) |
| Buy box inner padding (from 1024px) | 24px (lg) |
| Space between the two columns of a car page (from 1024px) | 32px (xl) |

Exceptions:

- Thumbnail size is 80px by 60px (4:3). Both are multiples of 4 and the whole thumbnail is a 60px-tall button, above the 44px minimum.
- Buy box column on a car page is 360px wide from 1024px (multiple of 4).
- Reserved action area: `min-height: 104px` (two 48px rows plus an 8px gap) on phones. Explained under Reserved action area.

## Color

Unchanged from Phase 1. **No new colour tokens are added** except one derived value:

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Scrim 60 (new, derived from Ink chrome) | `rgb(14 14 16 / 0.6)` | Photo counter pill in the gallery and lightbox. Becomes solid `#0E0E10` under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` |

Surface split for Phase 2 pages stays 60 / 30 / 10: page `#0E0E10` dominant, `#1A1A1E` cards (buy box from 1024px, spec table, key fact tiles, sold banner, share menu, chips) secondary, accent `#D0111A` for the list below. The lightbox is a full-ink surface like the page, with photos only (no logo).

**Sold badge colour (decision):** Off-white (`#F5F5F7`) fill, ink (`#0E0E10`) text, full pill. It is not accent, because accent means "act" and a sold car offers no action. It is not destructive, because sold is not an error. Off-white on a photo or on a dark card is the highest-contrast neutral in the palette (about 18:1).

Accent reserved for, extending the Phase 1 list (and nothing else):

1. The primary button fill: Phase 1 uses plus **Call now** (car page), **See more {Make}** (sold banner, 410 page), **See all used cars** (landing empty state and 404 page, when it is the single action)
2. The checked state of checkboxes, radios and selected chips, with a white check icon. **Includes the new "Include sold cars" checkbox**
3. The numeric badge on the "Filters" button (the new "Include sold cars" option counts as one applied filter)
4. The active page marker in pagination

One accent fill per view: Call now and See more {Make} never appear together (a sold page has no Call now).

Not accent: link chips on landing pages (card fill, control border), the Sold badge, the Share button (secondary), prices, breadcrumb links (text colour, underlined), carousel and lightbox controls, the active thumbnail ring.

Sold card behaviour: photos are never dimmed or greyed. The badge and the missing price carry the message.

## Layout

Mobile first, one column is the base. Breakpoints as Phase 1 (640, 1024, 1280).

### Car page `/used-cars/{slug}`

DOM order (also the mobile order, and the reading order for screen readers):

1. Back link (existing `BackToCars`, "Back to used cars", 44px tall)
2. **Gallery** (main carousel, from 1024px a thumbnail strip below it)
3. **Buy box**: Sold badge (sold cars only), title, price, Share, key facts, reserved action area
4. About this car
5. Specifications
6. Equipment
7. Similar cars

Phone (base): single column, 24px between gallery and buy box, 48px between later sections. Buy box has no card chrome on a phone (sits on the page surface).

From 1024px: two columns, `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr) 360px`, 32px gap. Gallery and sections 4 to 6 sit in column 1. The buy box sits in column 2, starting at row 1 and spanning the rows of column 1 (`row-span`), `position: sticky; top: 24px; align-self: start`. It becomes a `#1A1A1E` card: hairline border, 16px radius, 24px padding, 16px gap between its blocks. Similar cars spans both columns, 48px below.

**Primary focal point:** the price (Display, 32px / 600, text primary), directly under the title in the buy box. A buyer decides on price after seeing the photo, so the photo is the first thing seen and the price is the first thing read. The title is Heading (20px) on purpose so it does not compete. No hero, banner or second emphasised element. The one accent fill in the buy box is Call now. On sold cars there is no price; the focal point is the Sold banner heading.

### Buy box, top to bottom

| Block | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Sold badge | Sold cars only. Above the title |
| Title | `h1`: `{make} {model} {variant}` via `carTitle`. Wraps freely, never clamped on the page |
| Price row | Price (left) and Share button (right, 44px high). Row wraps if narrow. Sold cars: no price, the row holds the Share button alone, left aligned |
| Key facts | Up to four tiles in a 2 by 2 grid (all breakpoints). Tile: `#1A1A1E`, hairline, 16px radius, 16px padding; label Meta secondary above value Body strong. Inside the lg card the tiles use the `#0E0E10` surface and no border. Priority order: Year, Mileage, Fuel, Transmission, then Body type, Engine, Colour fill a missing slot. Show only tiles that have a value, never a blank tile |
| Action area | Below. See next section |

### Reserved action area (D-13)

- A block directly under the key facts with `min-height: 104px` on phones (two 48px rows plus 8px gap). From 1024px the buy box is a sticky card and the min-height is not needed (nothing below it can shift).
- Phase 2 content: row 1 only, a full-width **Call now** primary button (48px, accent fill, white label, phone icon on the left, 12px radius). Row 2 is empty. Phase 3 puts Enquire beside Call now (two columns in row 1) and Test drive and Reserve in row 2, so adding them moves nothing below the area.
- From 1024px, under the button, show the phone number as Meta secondary text, because desktop browsers cannot dial. The number comes from one site constant (`DEALER_PHONE`). The planner must take the number published on cartwright.ie and not invent one. Until confirmed, the constant is a clearly marked placeholder and the build must not ship it to production.
- Sold cars: the area shows the **Sold banner** in the same box (below) and no Call now.

### Sold banner (D-05)

A `#1A1A1E` card, hairline border, 16px radius, 16px padding, 8px between lines, same min-height as the action area on phones:

1. Heading: `Sold on {date}`
2. Meta, secondary: `This car is no longer for sale.`
3. Primary button (accent, 48px): `See more {Make}`, links to `/used-cars/{makeSlug}`

Date format: `3 November 2026` (`en-IE`, day, long month, year, **formatted in UTC** so the ISO date never shifts a day). The long form is used in the banner, the 410 page and share text. Sold cards and sold meta descriptions use the short form `3 Nov 2026`.

### Sections below the buy box

| Section | Content | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| About this car | The dealer's description as plain text | Rendered as text, never as HTML. `white-space: pre-line`, max 65ch. Omitted when absent |
| Specifications | `<dl>` of present fields only | `#1A1A1E` card, hairline, 16px radius. One column on phones; two columns from 640px (24px column gap). Each row: label left, value right of it, 1px hairline under every row, row padding 12px 0 inside a 16px card padding. Row order below. Omitted fields are not rendered |
| Equipment | Feature list | `<ul>`, one column on phones, two from 640px, each item a 16px check icon plus Body text. First 12 items visible; the rest sit inside a native `<details>` whose summary reads `Show all {n}` closed and `Show fewer` open (summary is a 44px-high control). Omitted when absent |
| Similar cars | Up to 3 cards (`ListingCard`) | Grid as the stock page (1, 2, 3 columns). Heading `Similar cars` and, at the right of the heading row, a text link `See all {Make}` to the make page. Selection (planner): in stock, same make first, then same body type, exclude the current car. Omitted when there are none |

Specifications row order (render only those with a value): Year, Mileage, Fuel, Transmission, Body type, Engine, Colour, Doors, Seats, Owners, NCT expires, Road tax, Max speed, 0 to 62 mph. Formats: engine `2.0 L` (one decimal, litres), NCT `Nov 2027` (month and year), max speed `210 km/h`, 0 to 62 `7.4 s`, owners and doors and seats as plain numbers, mileage via `formatKm`. The planner confirms what "tax due" means (annual amount or due date) from the 3-car trial output and labels the row to match; if it cannot be told apart, drop the row (D-10: nothing invented). Fields beyond this list are not rendered.

## Gallery (D-12)

Reuses the Phase 1 carousel pattern (native scroll-snap, `overscroll-behavior-x: contain`, hidden scrollbar, each slide `flex: 0 0 100%`, `scroll-snap-align: start`, no library). Differences from the card carousel:

| Part | Rule |
|------|------|
| Frame | `aspect-ratio: 4 / 3`, `#26262B` behind photos, 16px radius, `overflow: hidden`, `object-fit: cover`. Box reserved before images load |
| Slide | A `<button type="button">` wrapping the image, opens the lightbox at that index. `aria-label="View photo {i} of {n} larger"`. Image `alt` is `{year} {make} {model}, photo {i} of {n}` (Phase 1). Slides keep the Phase 1 group semantics (`role="group"`, `aria-roledescription="slide"`, `aria-label="Photo {i} of {n}"`) and the scroller keeps the carousel group semantics with `aria-label="Photos of {year} {make} {model}"` |
| Loading | Slide 1 is eager with `fetchpriority="high"` (it is the page's LCP). Others lazy with `decoding="async"`. 900 by 675 dimensions on every image. Image fades in over 150ms once loaded (Phase 1 ref-callback `settle` approach, not `onLoad`/`onError` props) |
| Counter | Pill at bottom right, 16px inset: `{i} / {n}` in Label, white on scrim 60 with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`. **No dots** (a real car can have 20+ photos). Hidden with one photo. `aria-hidden="true"` (the slide labels already announce position) |
| Arrows | Same as Phase 1 card arrows: 44px circle, white 85% with blur, hover-capable devices only, 8px from each edge, shown on gallery hover and `:focus-within`, `aria-disabled` and invisible at the ends but kept mounted. Labels "Previous photo" and "Next photo" |
| Thumbnail strip | From 1024px only, below the frame, 8px gap above. 80 by 60 buttons, 8px gap, 12px radius, one row that scrolls sideways inside its own box with snap (hidden scrollbar). Inactive thumb at 100% opacity with no ring; **active thumb has a 2px `#F5F5F7` ring with 2px offset** (not colour alone: also `aria-current="true"`). `aria-label="Show photo {i}"`. Click scrolls the main frame to that slide (`scrollTo`, smooth; instant under reduced motion). The active thumb is scrolled into view as the main frame moves. Thumbs are lazy and reuse the same files as the slides, so they hit the cache |
| Sold | Sold badge at top left, 16px inset (see Sold badge). Photos stay full colour |
| No photos | The 4:3 `#26262B` box with "No photo yet" (Meta, centred). No counter, arrows, thumbs or lightbox |
| One photo | No counter, no arrows, no thumbs. Tap still opens the lightbox (without arrows) |
| Image fails | Box stays, no broken-image icon (Phase 1 `data-failed`) |

### Lightbox

A native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`, class `lightbox`.

| Part | Rule |
|------|------|
| Surface | Full viewport (`100dvw` by `100dvh`), solid `#0E0E10`, no radius, no padding. Backdrop is the same ink, so no rim shows. Page behind does not scroll (extend the existing `body:has(dialog.sheet[open])` rule to `dialog.lightbox`) |
| Content | Its own scroll-snap scroller, same slide pattern, one photo per slide, `object-fit: contain`, centred, opens scrolled to the tapped index. Images are the same 900 by 675 files (the only size that exists); they fill the viewport width up to 1200px and are never stretched beyond that |
| Close | 44px circular button top right, 16px inset, white 85% with blur, ink close icon, `aria-label="Close photos"`. Receives initial focus (`autofocus`). Escape also closes. Focus returns to the slide button that opened it |
| Counter | Top left, 16px inset, same pill as the gallery (`{i} / {n}`), here `aria-live="polite"` is not used (slide labels announce) |
| Arrows | Previous and Next, same 44px circles, vertically centred, 16px from each edge, on hover-capable devices only. ArrowLeft and ArrowRight keys move one photo on every device |
| Label | `aria-label="Photos of {year} {make} {model}"` on the dialog; each slide labelled `Photo {i} of {n}` |
| Motion | Opens and closes with a 200ms opacity fade, `ease-out` (a full-screen takeover has no meaningful edge to slide from). Transition both ways, interruptible. Reduced motion: 150ms opacity, no change in property. Swipe down to dismiss is out of scope (see UI Considerations) |

## Share (D-15)

A client component with a 44px secondary button, label `Share` with the share icon on the left. Secondary button: `#1A1A1E` fill, 1px `#8A8A94` border, text `#F5F5F7`, 12px radius, 16px horizontal padding, press scale per Phase 1.

| Situation | Behaviour |
|-----------|-----------|
| `navigator.share` exists | Click calls it with `{ title: "{year} {make} {model}", text: "{year} {make} {model}, {price}", url }`. Sold cars: no price in `text`. If the buyer cancels (AbortError) nothing happens, no message |
| No `navigator.share` | Click toggles a **share menu**: a `#1A1A1E` panel, hairline border, 16px radius, `shadow-lift`, 8px padding, anchored under the button and right-aligned, with two 44px items (Body strong): `Share on WhatsApp` (a link to `https://wa.me/?text={encoded text + " " + url}`, `target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`) and `Copy link` (a button). Opens with a 150ms opacity plus 4px upward move (`ease-out`); reduced motion: opacity only. Closes on Escape, on outside click, on choosing an item, and returns focus to Share |
| Copy succeeds | The item label becomes `Link copied` for 2 seconds in an `aria-live="polite"` region, then the menu closes after that interval |
| Copy fails (clipboard blocked) | The item label becomes `Could not copy the link`. The WhatsApp item still works |
| Before JavaScript | Server render outputs the same WhatsApp link, labelled `Share`, styled as the button. Hydration swaps it for the real control with `useSyncExternalStore` (the pattern `BackToCars` uses), so there is no mismatch and the page shares without JS |
| WhatsApp text | `{year} {make} {model}, {price}. {url}`. Sold: `{year} {make} {model}. {url}`. Price via `formatPrice`. `url` is the canonical car URL, never the current query string |

No other share targets (Facebook, X, email) in v1.

## Listing card changes (D-04, D-14)

### "See all {n} photos" tile (D-14)

A final slide in every card carousel that has **2 or more photos**. Cards with one photo get no tile (a tile that leads to the same single photo adds a tap and no information). Cards with no photos are unchanged.

| Part | Rule |
|------|------|
| Slide | Same size and snap as photo slides. `role="group"`, `aria-roledescription="slide"`, `aria-label="See all {n} photos"`. It is **not** counted in "Photo i of n": real photos keep `Photo {i} of {n}` |
| Surface | `#26262B` (photo background), nothing on it but the content below |
| Content | Centred stack, 8px gap: a 44px circle filled `#F5F5F7` with an ink chevron-right icon, then the text `See all {n} photos` (Label, text primary, centred, wraps to 2 lines max) |
| Link | The circle and text are one real `<a>` to the car page, **in the tab order** (unlike photo slides, which stay `tabindex="-1"`). Visible focus ring per Phase 1. `n` is the car's full photo count, even if the card shows only its first 6 |
| Dots | Dots still count real photos only. While the tile is the visible slide, no dot is active |
| Arrows | Next arrow leads to the tile; the tile is the last slide, so the Next arrow is disabled there. The scroll-index maths treats the tile as index `n` |
| Motion | None added. It behaves like any slide. No bounce, no highlight |

Files touched: `PhotoCarousel.tsx` (add the slide, index range, end state), `ListingCard.tsx` (pass the car link and total).

### Sold card (D-04)

Sold cars appear in the stock list only when `sold=1`.

- Sold badge on the photo, top left (see Sold badge).
- The price line is replaced by `Sold {d MMM yyyy}`, for example `Sold 3 Nov 2026`, in the Heading role, tabular numbers, **secondary text colour** (`#A1A1AA`). Same 20px line so card height and grid alignment do not change.
- Title and meta lines unchanged. The card still links to the car page. The carousel and tile work as for any card.
- No dimming, no grayscale, no opacity change on the card.

### Stock page: Include sold cars (D-04)

| Part | Rule |
|------|------|
| Control | A single checkbox row, label `Include sold cars`, as the last filter group, after Mileage, separated by a 1px hairline. Same markup and checked style as other checkboxes (accent fill, white check). No count next to it |
| Placement | Both the sidebar (from 1024px) and the filter sheet, from the same `StockFilters` component |
| URL | `sold=1`. Absent when off. Any other value is ignored |
| Applied chip | When on, a chip `Sold cars included` with the Phase 1 remove icon appears with the other chips. It counts toward the Filters button badge. `Clear all filters` clears it |
| Counts | Facet counts and the "Show n cars" button follow the Phase 1 rule but **count in-stock cars only unless `sold=1`**. Turning the toggle on changes counts and totals; turning it off restores them |
| Order | With `sold=1`, sold cars always list after all in-stock cars, and within each group follow the chosen sort. The toggle never pushes sold cars in front of cars the buyer can buy |

## Landing pages `/used-cars/{make}`, `/used-cars/{body}`, `/used-cars/{make}/{model}` (D-06 to D-09)

One page layout for all three. Server rendered, no client components except what `ListingCard` already has.

Top to bottom:

1. **Breadcrumb** (Meta, 44px tall links): `Used cars` > `{Make}` > `{Model}`. Separator is a 16px chevron-right (`aria-hidden`). The last item is plain text with `aria-current="page"`. `<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb"><ol>`. Wraps, never scrolls sideways
2. **`h1`** (Display), 24px below the breadcrumb: `Used {Make}`, `Used {Body} cars`, `Used {Make} {Model}`
3. **Intro paragraph** (Body, 65ch), 16px below
4. **Link chips** row, 24px below (see next table)
5. **Result count** (Meta) and the **grid** of `ListingCard` (same columns, gaps and priority loading as the stock page: first two cards are `priority`), at most 12 cards, newest first. In-stock cars only
6. If the total is more than 12: a secondary button `See all {n} {Make}` (or `{Body} cars`, `{Make} {Model}`) to the stock list with the matching filter (`/used-cars?make=audi`, `?body=suv`, `?make=audi&model=a4`), 48px high, 32px below the grid. No pagination on landing pages
7. Second chip row where the table says so (Body types on a make page)

No search, sort or filter controls on landing pages. They are entry points, the stock page is the tool.

### Intro template (D-07: live facts only, no invented text)

| Page | Paragraph |
|------|-----------|
| Make | `We have {n} used {Make} in stock in Tralee, from {min} to {max}.` Second sentence: `Models in stock: {A3, A4, Q5}.` (comma list, model names only, all of them) |
| Body type | `We have {n} used {Body} cars in stock in Tralee, from {min} to {max}.` Second sentence: `Makes in stock: {Audi, BMW}.` |
| Make and model | `We have {n} used {Make} {Model} in stock in Tralee, from {min} to {max}.` Second sentence: `Years: {oldest} to {newest}.` (a single year reads `Year: {year}.`) |

Rules: `{n}` of 1 reads `We have 1 used {Make} in stock in Tralee, at {price}.` and drops "from and to". When min and max are equal, use `at {price}`. Money via `formatPrice`. A body type with one car reads `1 used {Body} car`. All figures are computed from the in-stock cars at request time.

### Link chips (D-09)

Chips here are links: pill, 44px high, `#1A1A1E` fill, 1px `#8A8A94` border, 16px horizontal padding, label in Body strong followed by the count in Meta secondary (`A4 6`, the Phase 1 facet format), 8px gap, wrapping rows, never a sideways scroller. Press scale per Phase 1. Labels clamp to one line with an ellipsis; the count never truncates. Only entries with at least one car in stock are rendered. These chips are never accent (they are navigation, not selection).

| Page | Under the intro | Under the grid |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| Make | `Models` (heading, then model chips to `/used-cars/{make}/{model}`) | `Body types` (chips to `/used-cars/{body}`) |
| Body type | `Makes` (chips to `/used-cars/{make}`) | none |
| Make and model | `More {Make}` (sibling model chips), then a text link `All {Make}` to the make page | none |

A section with no entries is not rendered (no empty heading).

### Empty state (D-08)

A known make, body type or make and model with no current stock renders the same page frame with the intro, the model/make chips and grid replaced by:

1. `h2` (Heading): `No {Make} in stock right now` / `No {Body} cars in stock right now` / `No {Make} {Model} in stock right now`
2. Body: `Have a look at these instead.`
3. Chips (same style) headed `Makes in stock` (make and make and model pages) or `Body types in stock` (body pages), only those with stock
4. **Recently sold**: only if sold cars of that make, body type or model exist inside the sold period. Heading `Recently sold {Make}` (`{Body} cars`, `{Make} {Model}`), then up to 3 `ListingCard`s with Sold badges and `Sold {date}`. Hidden entirely when there are none
5. Primary button (accent, 48px): `See all used cars`

Phase 3 adds `Cannot find it? Ask us to find it for you.` with the sourcing form link above the button (same line as the Phase 1 stock empty state). Do not add it now.

The page is `noindex` and left out of the sitemap (D-08). It still returns 200.

## Gone and not found (D-03)

Both use the normal page frame: header, 48px top space, `h1` Display, Body text 65ch, buttons 24px below.

| Page | Heading | Body | Actions |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| 410 (a sold car past `SOLD_VISIBLE_DAYS`, or a removed car) | `This car is no longer available` | `It has been sold or taken off our site. Here is what else we have.` | Primary `See more {Make}` to the make page, then text link `See all used cars` to `/used-cars` (D-03) |
| 404 (a slug the provider never had) | `We could not find that page` | `The link may be old or mistyped.` | Primary `See all used cars` |

The make is known for 410 because the slug prefix is the make slug and the expired car is still in the provider (D-03). If the make has no stock, `See more {Make}` still works because the make page renders its empty state. These pages are `noindex`. Next.js serves the body through whatever mechanism returns status 410; the planner reads `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` for how. The contract above is the body regardless of mechanism.

## Metadata copy (D-07, D-17)

The root layout already adds ` | Carright` through `title.template`. **Page titles below must not repeat it.** No em dashes. Keep titles under 60 characters before the suffix.

| Page | Title | Meta description |

## Accessibility baseline for this phase

Phase 5 owns the full WCAG 2.2 AA pass; this phase must not create debt. Phase 1 baseline still applies. Additionally:

- One `h1` per page. Landing and car pages use a logical heading order (h1, then h2 for sections).
- Gallery slide buttons, thumbs, arrows, Share, menu items, tile link and chips are all native `button` or `a` elements with visible names or `aria-label`s.
- The lightbox is a modal `<dialog>` with `showModal()`: focus is trapped, Escape closes, focus returns to the opener. ArrowLeft and ArrowRight work on every device.
- Selected thumbnail uses `aria-current="true"` plus a ring, never colour alone. The Sold badge is text.
- Share menu is reachable and operable by keyboard; Link copied is announced through an `aria-live="polite"` region.
- Sold badge ink on white: 19:1. Secondary text `#A1A1AA` on white and on `#F5F5F7` meets 4.5:1. Counter pill white on scrim 60 over a photo is not guaranteed against a bright photo, so the pill carries the `backdrop-filter` blur and a 60% ink floor; under reduced transparency it is solid ink (19:1).
- Breadcrumb is a labelled `nav` with an ordered list and `aria-current="page"`.
- Target size: all controls 44px minimum, primary buttons 48px.
