| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-04-11 | Tampering | `GOOGLE_PLACE_ID` and address in URLs | low | mitigate | Every query value passes through `encodeURIComponent` (unit test) |
